Three numbers, computed and shown

  • Multiple days: 3 nights are charged, not one. The bill shows “3 nights × 2 guests” so the total is never a mystery.
  • Multiple guests: the nightly rate is charged per guest. Two guests means two beds and two lots of ₹350.

Both paths still need your approval. Paying — either way — does not confirm the booking. That is your rule.
